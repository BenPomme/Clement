# Surface brief: home (/) and service pages

Scope: marketing site, Persuade mode. Audience: Mulhouse-area households (families, new parents, seniors & caregivers, special diets). Action: send a quote request (form) or open WhatsApp. Proof on hand: 12 real dish photos; no testimonials yet (never invent). Constraints: French, static, GitHub Pages, CNIL consent, tax-credit claims "sous réserve" until SAP number exists.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A week of meals, already done, laid out on the household's own linen. Refuses the category default (stock chef smiling over a cream page with three icon cards).

OWN-WORLD: Alsatian kelsch: woven linen check in deep kelsch blue (#22408f family) over cool linen white, with the darker crossing squares of a real weave; madder red used once per viewport as a thread/stamp. Dishes appear as cut-out plates lying on cloth; masking-tape labels in marker hand name day + dish + portions. Bricolage Grotesque display, Atkinson Hyperlegible body (senior legibility), Shantell Sans only on tape labels.

STORY: Visitor sees real food, understands "a cook comes to my kitchen and fills my fridge for the week", sees exact price and price after tax credit, picks household size, sends request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed kelsch-blue woven field. Left: headline "Votre semaine, cuisinée chez vous." at display scale, one-line subhead. Right: two or three cut-out plates on the cloth with tape labels. Bottom band of the field: the semainier: household-size selector [1–2][3][4][5+], Mon→Sun strip filling with meals, live price + after-credit price, primary button "Recevoir mon menu et mon devis". WhatsApp in the nav.

FORM: seed: none (pinned): the user chose "Kelsch & semainier" from four presented directions (its card specified the kelsch world and the semainier first viewport). concept-seed was not run in this session (launcher not executed), so no roll took place.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the semainier strip fills day by day (staggered, ease-out) when the household size or formula changes. Motion grammar: things are laid down on cloth: short drop with soft shadow settle; nothing floats.
