## Users

Households in the Mulhouse area (Haut-Rhin, Alsace) who want a week of home-cooked meals without cooking them:
- busy working families (core market), including cross-border workers employed in Switzerland but tax-resident in France;
- new parents / post-partum households;
- seniors and their caregiving relatives;
- people on specific diets (gluten-free, vegetarian, low-salt, diabetic, high-protein for sport).

They find the service mostly on Google ("batch cooking Mulhouse", "cuisinier à domicile Mulhouse") and through local ads, on a phone, often in the evening. Their job: get a fridge full of good, varied, labelled meals for the week, at a price they can justify, from someone they trust in their kitchen.

## Product Purpose

La Cuisine de Clément: Clément Bahr, a professional cook, comes to the client's home and batch-cooks the household's meals for the week in the client's own kitchen (optionally doing the grocery shopping first), then leaves the kitchen clean with labelled containers in the fridge. Success = qualified quote requests / WhatsApp conversations from the Mulhouse area, measured by ads tracking.

## Positioning

A trained restaurant cook (not a home-cook side hustle), cooking restaurant-level dishes from his own repertoire, priced about 10% below the established local batch-cooking competitors. Competitors' look and copy are not to be copied.

## Operating Context

- Service area: Mulhouse and agglomeration (Rixheim, Riedisheim, Illzach, Kingersheim, Wittenheim, Brunstatt-Didenheim, Habsheim, Pfastatt, Lutterbach, Sausheim, Wittelsheim) plus Saint-Louis, Huningue, Kembs, Sierentz, Altkirch, Cernay, Thann, Guebwiller.
- Process benchmarks in the category: discovery call → menu chosen/proposed (J-2 to J-4) → shopping list or chef shopping → 3–4 h session → labelled meals, reheating notes, kitchen cleaned.
- Tax: home meal preparation (including shopping time) is eligible for the 50% "services à la personne" tax credit (service-public.gouv.fr F12). Groceries are not eligible. Swiss/German residents are not eligible.
- Language: French only.

## Product Principles

1. Sell the outcome (a full fridge for the week), not the hours.
2. Price transparency: show the price, the price after tax credit, and what's included, with no surprises.
3. Trust before persuasion: a real person, real food, real kitchen; no invented proof.
4. Every visit should end in one easy action: describe your household → get a menu and quote.
5. Local first: Mulhouse-area words, towns and context everywhere it matters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Seniors and their families are a target segment: generous text size, high contrast, large tap targets, plain French.
